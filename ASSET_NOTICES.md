## GNU deck

The 41 PNG files in `public/cards/gnu/` are Trucoshi's web adaptation of
**Baraja española.svg**. Trucoshi rasterized and split the source
SVG into individual cards, retained the 40 ranks used by Truco plus the card
back, and omitted ranks 8 and 9 and the combined sheet. The adaptation is
licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
